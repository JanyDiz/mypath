Research for **mypath** (AI interview prep for designers & PMs), structured in **three levels**. Collected June 2026 via live page fetch (WebFetch) + Playwright screenshots ([`screens/`](./screens/)), built on an earlier multi-source, adversarially-verified web study.

## TL;DR across all three

- **Niche validated, not empty.** Two hard competitors (Mockin, UXMock.io); soft tier (Exponent, Yoodli, Google Warmup, ADPList); aspirational bar set by Grammarly / Speak / Duolingo.
- **The moat is depth, not novelty.** Rubrics already exist — but no AI tool scores above 2/4 on *seniority of judgment* or nails *format-specificity*. The win = AI feedback *craft* (Grammarly) × human-coach *judgment* (ADPList). Target ≥30/32 on the feedback benchmark.
- **Don't fight UXMock on live voice.** They own real-time + design challenge. Lead **async, depth-first, portfolio walkthrough + behavioral**.
- **Core loop:** async record → deep rubric report, shown as inline margin critique (Pattern C + D). Real-time voice deferred to premium phase 2.

## Pricing & framework anchors (carried from the prior study)

- AI tools cluster **$8–25/mo**; free tiers everywhere; human coaching **$100–300/session** = value anchor. Positioning: **~$12–15/mo + discounted annual + job-cycle one-time pass**.
- Rubric anchors: **NN/g heuristic evaluation** (justify every critique against a named principle) for design challenge; **CIRCLES Method** (7 steps + 6 eval criteria) for PM cases. Both are *answer-structuring* frameworks, not validated scoring rubrics — adapting them is mypath's senior-judgment layer.
